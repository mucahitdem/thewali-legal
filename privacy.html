Privacy Policy

TheWali Publisher · Effective date: 17 October 2026

TheWali Publisher ("the app") is a small desktop tool used by simgameslab, the developer of the game The Wali, to upload its own promotional videos to its own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube and Instagram). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Information the app accesses

  • Account authorization tokens issued by TikTok, Google/YouTube or Instagram after the account owner signs in and approves access.
  • Basic profile information returned by those platforms (such as account ID and display name), used only to confirm which account a video is uploaded to.
  • Video files and captions created by simgameslab, which the app uploads at the owner's request.

How the information is used

The information is used only to upload and publish simgameslab's own videos to simgameslab's own accounts, and to check the status of those uploads. The app does not read, collect or analyse other users' content, followers, messages or personal data.

Storage and sharing

  • Tokens are stored only on the developer's own computer and are never sent anywhere except to the platform that issued them.
  • No information is sold, rented or shared with third parties, and nothing is used for advertising or tracking.
  • The app has no servers, analytics or cookies.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the app is in use and can be deleted at any time by removing them from the developer's computer. Access can also be revoked from each platform's settings (TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions; Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites).

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly process children's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
